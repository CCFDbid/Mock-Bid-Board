How to use the bid board

A tool for running CCFD's annual station bid — place people into seats, shift by shift. Everyone gets their own board, so nothing you do affects anyone else.

Placing someone Whoever is up next is already selected — their name shows in the amber pill up top. Tap the seat you want them in. Tap any name in the roster to bid out of order.
Moving / removing Tap someone already on the board to pick them up, then tap where they go. The ✕ on a seat clears it. Filled seats are lighter than empty ones, and a seat flashes when someone lands in it.
Tall vs Wide Tall is the standard board — three shift columns side by side, and the one to bid from on your phone. Wide turns it sideways: shifts become rows (A/B/C down the left) with the rigs running across, so far more of the bid fits at once. Wide is the one to use in landscape or mirrored to a TV.
The two buttons: ▤ and ⤢ ⤢ hides the bars completely so the board gets the whole screen — tap again to bring them back. Who's on the clock stays visible either way. ▤ hides the PM/RS/AC counts, which are the part that reads as clutter from across a room. Shift letters and BCs stay put.
Which shift is which Each column and row is outlined in its own color — A red, B blue, C green.
Floats, Training and Holding Floats sit with their shift. In Wide, Training and Holding are below the fold — scroll down to reach them, so the three shifts get the whole screen.
Seeing more Fit fits the width, Fit All fits the whole bid on screen, and −/+ zoom. Your zoom sticks when you hide the bars. In Tall, tap a shift letter (A/B/C) to blow that shift up; tap again to go back. Wide is a fixed one-screen layout, so it has no zoom.
The roster Show Roster opens it. ⇄ Shift slides it across so it sits over A, then B, then C. The small button next to AC hides the search bar and count for more room.
Why won't someone drop in? The rules are built in: captains only in captain seats, only AC-qualified firefighters can act as captain, the Paramedic seat needs a medic, and every rig has to get a medic before anyone doubles up.
Balance Paramedics (in ⋯ Menu) keeps medics even across the three shifts and tells you which shift to bid instead. It steps aside when a rig has no medic yet — covering every rig comes first.
Roster changes Edit Roster (in ⋯ Menu) — add someone as Last, First with their rank, and tap PM / RS / AC on any row to turn codes on or off for people already on the list.
Sharing Copy Share Link gives a link that opens your exact bid. Copy as Text gives a plain list you can paste in a group text.
Full screen on your phone Open the board in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Launching from that icon drops the address bar and tabs entirely — with ⤢ it's all board.

Reset board?

This clears every name off the board. Your roster stays as-is.

Save Bid

Copy this bid code (Notes, email to yourself) or download it. To restore later use Load Bid. Saves the whole board and roster.

Load Bid

Paste a saved bid code and tap Apply — or choose a saved .json file. Replaces the current board.

Edit Roster

Add or remove people. Type names as Last, First. Removing someone placed clears them off the board. Tick the codes (PM / RS / AC) when you add someone.
